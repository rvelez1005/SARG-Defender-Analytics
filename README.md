# SARG-Defender-Analytics
In Soccer, and like in most sports, it is often a challenge to effectively and accurately quantify the true impact a defensive player (Fullbacks, Center backs, and Defensive-Midfielders) has on a team. It is also often the case that offensive players (Atticking Midfielders, Wingers, Strikers) get most of the recognition, resulting in defensive players taking on the role of unsung heroes. This is backed by the fact that, excluding this year, the last time a defensive player won the Ballon D'or, Soccer's most prestigious individual award, was in 2006 by Center Back Fabio Cannavaro. Moreover, only five different defensive players have EVER won the award, Franz Beckenbauer (1972, 1976), Lothar Matthaus (1990), Matthias Sammer (1996), Fabio Cannavaro (2006), and Rodri (2024). Our goal is to change this. We are aiming to create accurate soccer defender ratings/rankings for scouting purposes and to properly quanitfy the impact of defensive players (individually) on their teams success.
